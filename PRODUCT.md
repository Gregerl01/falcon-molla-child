# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Contractors, dealers, municipal/government fleets, commercial upfitters, and field
service businesses evaluating and specifying truck bodies. They arrive to understand
body categories, compare configurations and technical specs, and request quotes or
find a dealer. Context is mixed desktop (dealers, fleet buyers at a desk) and mobile
(contractors and field staff in the yard).

## Product Purpose

Falcon Truck Bodies marketing site plus WooCommerce product catalog. It exists to help
buyers understand the truck body categories (dump, chipper, platform, welder, saw,
service/contractor/mechanics bodies, haulers), compare products and specs, and convert
to a quote request or dealer contact. Success = a buyer can confidently identify the
right body line and start a quote/dealer conversation.

## Brand Personality

Premium industrial. Durable, precise, fleet-ready, technical, trustworthy. The design
language is clean dark industrial surfaces, Inter typography, a single blue accent
(#208BF4), strong product photography, and reduced visual clutter. Voice is confident
and specification-driven, not gimmicky.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS/startup landing aesthetics; cream/sand "warm editorial" palettes; gradient
text; glassmorphism; identical icon-card grids; decorative clutter that competes with
product photography. Should not feel like a template demo.

## Design Principles

- Photography-first: product imagery carries the brand; chrome stays out of its way.
- One accent, used deliberately: blue #208BF4 for emphasis and action, not decoration.
- Spec-driven confidence: surface real configuration/technical detail, not filler copy.
- Reduced clutter: dark industrial surfaces and generous spacing over busy ornament.
- Convert to quote/dealer: every category path leads toward comparison and contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA where practical. Priorities: text contrast (body and on-image overlays),
visible button/link focus states, readable body copy at the small base size, and mobile
usability (touch targets, no horizontal overflow).
